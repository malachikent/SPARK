1. In this experiment, 0.246 g of camphor was produced from borneol through reflux
distillation. The percent yield calculated suggests that a large amount of product was lost during
reflux. Further analysis could be taken to determine the composition of the product and why
material was lost. Possible sources of error include pipetting the wrong layer, not refluxing long
enough, and overheating the mixture during reflux. Of the sources of error mentioned, the one to
have most likely occurred would be pipetting the wrong layer. Pipetting the right layer is
determined by density. The product with the larger density sits on the bottom, so the product’s
density should be known in order to extract the correct layer. One way to resolve this error is to
write down the densities of the reactant and product before starting the experiment.
